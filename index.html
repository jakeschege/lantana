The story

Named for a flower.
Built for stillness.

Inspired by the colourful Lantana camara that flourishes across these slopes, Lantana Homestay Cabins celebrates the beauty and charm of its natural surroundings.

A peaceful homestay near the foothills of Mount Kenya in Naromoru, pairing comfortable private accommodation with warm hospitality, home-style dining, and easy access to guided nature excursions.
